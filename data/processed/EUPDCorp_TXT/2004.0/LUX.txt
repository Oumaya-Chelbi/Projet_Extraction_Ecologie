progress towards european union membership is to be seen in two ways: the political class's willingness to change and the capacity to put it into practice. the proposal to delay romania's admission by the very same people who were in favour of turkey this morning gives me a problem. i think that's very arrogant. only 15 years have passed since the romanian revolution, and it could have just as easily ended in civil war. so it is to be hoped that president basescu will succeed in forming a coalition that is concerned with the national interest rather than the profile of the various political parties. and if president iliescu and president constantinescu, who was a member of the european people's party, have not succeeded in bringing about all the necessary reforms, it must still be acknowledged that they have ensured relative institutional stability in the country. so instead of playing the teacher, let us have some respect for the efforts already made and give due weight to those that remain to be accomplished. mr president, 2007 will be a great moment for romania anyway, because in 2007 a romanian city, sibiu, will be, together with luxembourg, the cultural capital of europe. it is a proposal of the luxembourg government, a joint decision of the council of ministers and the commission. the rich romanian heritage, which is multicultural and multiethnic, will certainly be an enrichment for the culture of europe, and romania should also be seen in terms of its cultural wealth and the potential of its people, who have been wounded by the madness of a dictator and the evils of a totalitarian ideology. mr president, i voted this morning to open negotiations with turkey. i shall be in favour of romania's admission and i shall try to see that we do everything we can to achieve this. i think our rapporteur has drawn the right conclusions from his report and i thank him for that.
