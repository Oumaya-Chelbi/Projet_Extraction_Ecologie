in writing. - (pt) we highlight four decisions of the european council in helsinki on 10 and 11 december which deserve some comment, regardless of subsequent considerations. - we note with apprehension the definition of the outlines of the next intergovernmental conference which may open the door to the implementation of 'guidelines' and the deepening of the eu's federalist orientation. - we regret that the council has decided to accept turkey as a candidate for membership, we hope at least that no further steps will be taken until the government of turkey has taken the necessary measures with regard to human rights, international law on the delimitation of frontiers, the application of un resolutions on cyprus and a political solution to the kurdish question. - we criticise the decision to develop the european union's crisis management capabilities in the context of a strengthened common european security and defence policy, designed to develop and strengthen the european pillar of nato, and we reject the militarist view of the use of war to resolve international conflicts. new initiatives must be taken to disarmament and the prohibition of nuclear tests. - finally, we consider it essential to pay greater attention to the quality of employment, the reduction of working time and the dignity of workers, and to put an end to the emphasis on flexibility of work and wage moderation.
in writing. - (pt) we voted against the joint motion for a resolution on the helsinki european council because of the rejection of amendments, notably those tabled by the uen group, which would improve the chapters on 'enlargement', 'intergovernmental conference' and 'external relations'. but we have expressly agreed with paragraphs 19 to 27 (competitive economy and employment) and 29 to 34 (environment, public health and the fight against organised crime and drugs). we welcome the fact that the european council has maintained and seems to want to maintain the intergovernmental method at the centre of the union's institutional system; and we are moving away from the blindness which leads some to claim everything for the european parliament by diminishing and obscuring the role of national parliaments in the european process - this is an unacceptable mistake. we also disagree with the haste with which turkey is being treated. on the external front, we particularly regret the absence of any mention of the very serious situation in angola, and the absence of any reservations about the agreement with mexico, which may favour the alena countries overall, by exacerbating inequality in reciprocal access to markets and seriously damaging the interests of european industries. we regret again the silence on russia, which will not allow the tone of certain manifest exaggerations in the resolution on chechnya to be corrected.
