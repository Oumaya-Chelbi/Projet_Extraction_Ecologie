(es) mr president, the commission has received broad support for the approaches in the green paper 'towards a european strategy for energy security' in relation to maintaining access to community coal reserves and maintaining a basic level of production for this purpose. in line with this idea, the commission proposed and the council adopted - with the european parliament's prior opinion - council regulation (ec) no 1407/2002 of 23 july 2002 on state aid to the coal industry. the objectives of this regulation differ from those mentioned by the honourable member in relation to minero siderúrgica de ponferrada, sa. coal companies receiving state aid have always had the obligation to strive for economic viability. in addition, an important new feature of the new council regulation is that the contribution of indigenous coal to the objective of energy security must be made in a sustainable development framework. the commission therefore considers that both open-air and underground farms can respect the principles of sustainable development insofar as they comply with community legislation.
